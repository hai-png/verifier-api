## Findings and fixes

| # | Finding (evidence) | Fix | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Valid-key auth = 2 sequential DB round trips.** Prisma `include: { workspace }` runs a second query. Server pipeline time was 0.2 ms, yet valid-key requests were ~170 ms slower than unknown-key ones live, and 124 vs 63 ms in the lab. | `utils/apiKeyLookup.ts`: one JOIN, with columns taken from the Prisma DMMF and values coerced to Prisma types. Falls back to the Prisma include on error. | Lab `verify_validate_400` **124 → 62 ms**. CI fails if the fallback ever fires. |
| 2 | **Quota charge = BEGIN/UPDATE/COMMIT (3 RTTs)**, holding the workspace row lock across 2 of them. The quota stage took 183 ms at c=1 and 1.7 s p95 at c=40, because every verification serialized on that row. | `chargeVerificationCredits` / `refundVerificationCredits`: one conditional autocommit `UPDATE … WHERE credits >= n`. Just as atomic, and the lock is held only for the statement. Prisma fallback. | Quota p50 **183 → 62 ms**. At c=40, quota p95 **1,731 → 196 ms**. |
| 3 | **Billing-config TTL expiry blocked requests.** Live `rate_limit` p90 158 ms, max 315 ms. | Stale-while-revalidate: one background refresh, a max-stale bound (5 min), and a generation guard so an admin update can't be overwritten by a stale load. | Unit tests: 20 concurrent stale reads return in under 20 ms with one refresh. |
| 4 | **Pool too small for the cross-region RTT.** `connection_limit=5` × ~6.5 q/s per connection ≈ 32 q/s. Live `/ready` and unknown-key p95 reached 650–770 ms at 10–25 workers while DB-free paths stayed under 160 ms. | `DB_CONNECTION_LIMIT` (render.yaml: 10) overrides the URL. A URL without a limit now gets 10 instead of Prisma's CPU-derived 3–5. | Unit tests. Lab throughput below. |
| 5 | **p95 spikes of 1.8–3.1 s at every concurrency step.** The lazy pool opened new TLS connections to the DB mid-burst. | The pool is warmed at boot, before the heavy stats aggregation. | Lab tails: c=5/10 p99 is now 572/549 ms. |
| 6 | **CPU ceiling ~155 RPS even for `/health`** (700 ms p50 at c=100). Each request wrote 2 colorized console lines, 3 on a 4xx. | One line per request. `/`, `/health` and `/ready` probes log at debug. 5xx logs at warn. | Final lab: c=40 **189 RPS** vs 36.6 before (together with #1, #2, #4). |
| 7 | **Security: the request logger wrote raw POST bodies** (references, phone numbers, account suffixes) **and `?apiKey=` secrets** to logs. | Bodies are no longer logged, only their byte size. `apiKey/key/token/secret` query values are redacted. | Unit test. ⚠️ Logs that already exist are not purged. If they are retained anywhere, review access, and consider rotating keys that were used via `?apiKey=`. |
| 8 | Stats maps (per path, per IP) grew without limit, and the finish handler assumed entries were never evicted. | Capped at `REQUEST_STATS_MAX_KEYS` (5,000), and the handler tolerates evicted entries. | Unit tests / typecheck. |
| 9 | `loadtest/README.md` had unresolved merge-conflict markers. | Resolved, keeping the session-path section. | — |

## Lab before → after (same topology, same scenario mix)

| Workers | RPS before | RPS after | p95 before | p95 after |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5.2 | 7.0 | 712 | 531 |
| 5 | 24.0 | 33.9 | 755 | 538 |
| 10 | 39.9 | 67.2 | 858 | 535 |
| 20 | 39.4 | 129.2 | 1,467 | 544 |
| 40 | 36.6 | **189.1** | 2,827 | **715** |

Uncached synthetic Telebirr p50 went from **715 → 533 ms**. About 400 ms of that is the stub provider, so app overhead fell from ~310 ms to ~130 ms. 0 errors in 6,620 requests. The mix includes public, auth-rejection and policy paths, so this is **not** "189 successful verifications/second". Real providers are slower and weren't load-tested.
